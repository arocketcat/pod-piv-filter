Cross check with independent PIV processors
===========================================

All three processors read the same 16 bit TIFFs (1000 pairs). POD filtering was done once, with
pod_filter.py on the TIFFs (10 modes per cavity), before any processor saw the images. Same pass
schedule everywhere: 64 px (step 32), then three 32 px passes (step 16).
